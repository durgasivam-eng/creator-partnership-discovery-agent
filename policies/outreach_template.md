# Approved Creator Outreach Template

## Use

Create a draft only for a creator selected in the proposed final five. Never send it.

## Personalization rule

Personalize only the opening sentence. It must refer to supported evidence and cite the source ID used. Do not infer parenthood, identity, family structure, or a child's characteristics. Do not invent praise.

## Template

Hi [display name]! [One warm, personalized sentence based on cited synthetic public evidence.]

I'm Durga, the founder of Vedha for Little Minds. We create children's books that help families introduce Tamil to young children, and I'd love to explore a possible collaboration around *My First 100 Tamil Words*.

If it feels like a good fit for your audience, I'd be happy to share more details for you to review. No pressure at all.

Thanks,
Durga

## Guardrails

- Write as a concise Instagram DM with no subject line.
- Keep the tone friendly, warm, and personable rather than formal or sales-heavy.
- Preserve the approved meaning and safety boundaries.
- Avoid em dashes.
- Do not comment on a child's appearance.
- Do not assume the creator is a parent or guardian.
- Do not make promises about payment, deliverables, shipping, timing, or partnership approval.
- Keep the result as a draft pending founder approval.
