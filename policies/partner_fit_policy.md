# Partner Fit Policy

## Campaign audience

The campaign should reach parents and guardians who may want to teach Tamil to young children.

## Direct product conflicts

A creator is Not Qualified when supported evidence shows that the account is primarily a children's book brand, publisher, retailer, or author promoting and selling its own competing Tamil children's books. Record `Direct product conflict`, cite the supporting source IDs, and do not rank or draft outreach for the account.

Do not infer a product conflict merely because an account reviews books, works in a library, teaches literacy, or discusses children's literature.

## Preferred partner types

Give strongest partnership preference to:

1. Parent or family creators who publicly state that role and regularly share Tamil, bilingual-parenting, young-child, or early-learning content.
2. Tamil teaching organizations, educators, cultural-learning programs, or community learning groups serving young children and families.

General book reviewers, librarians, lifestyle creators, and broad cultural creators may qualify only when supported evidence shows meaningful access to the target parent or early-learning audience and no direct product conflict.

## Scoring priority

Reserve Product and Campaign Fit scores of 26-30 for clearly supported parent or family creators and Tamil teaching or early-learning organizations with direct relevance to *My First 100 Tamil Words*. Other eligible account types may receive no more than 25 in this category.

When total scores are equal or within 3 points, prefer the publicly supported parent or family creator or Tamil teaching organization before applying the remaining tie-breakers.

## Evidence standard

Every partner-type preference and product-conflict decision must cite the exact synthetic source IDs used. Record unsupported facts as `Unknown`; never infer parenthood, organizational purpose, authorship, product ownership, or competitive status.
