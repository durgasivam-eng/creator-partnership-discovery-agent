# Partnership Fit Scoring Rules

Score only creators classified as Qualified. The maximum score is 100.

## Product and campaign fit - 30 points

- 26-30: Direct, recurring fit with Tamil early-learning books and children roughly ages 2-7.
- 20-25: Strong fit with children's literacy, bilingual learning, or cultural learning; Tamil is meaningfully present.
- 12-19: Adjacent family or book content with limited direct campaign fit.
- 0-11: Weak fit. A score this low should normally indicate the creator is not Qualified.

## Tamil and cultural alignment - 25 points

- 22-25: Tamil language or culture is an explicit, recurring primary theme.
- 16-21: Tamil content appears regularly but shares focus with broader themes.
- 8-15: Tamil relevance is occasional but supported.
- 0-7: Little or no supported Tamil alignment. A score this low should normally indicate the creator is not Qualified.

## Audience relevance - 20 points

- 17-20: Strong focus on young children, early literacy, parents/guardians, or early-years education.
- 12-16: Regular family, children's books, or educational content.
- 6-11: Some relevant content but the dominant audience is older or broader.
- 0-5: Little supported audience relevance.

## Collaboration readiness - 15 points

- 13-15: Explicit partnership invitation or media kit plus relevant disclosed collaborations.
- 9-12: Public business contact or review policy with a clear collaboration signal.
- 4-8: Contact route exists but readiness is weak or indirect.
- 0-3: No supported collaboration signal.

## Geographic diversity - 10 points

- 10: Country is not yet represented among locked Approved creators in the current final five.
- 6: Country is represented once.
- 3: Country is represented twice.
- 0: Country is represented three or more times.

## Ranking and tie-breakers

Rank the complete eligible Qualified pool from highest to lowest total score. Resolve equal totals in this order:

1. Higher Product and Campaign Fit score.
2. Higher Tamil and Cultural Alignment score.
3. Higher Geographic Diversity score.
4. Lower follower count.
5. Earlier original input-file order.

Needs Human Verification may receive a clearly labelled provisional score, but it must not enter the final ranking. Not Qualified creators receive no ranking score.
