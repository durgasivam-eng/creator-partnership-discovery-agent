      Known limitations
      • Handles only: the prototype evaluates the ordered, founder-approved Instagram-style handles embedded in the file. It does not discover creators, scrape Instagram, or search the live web.
      • English-language evidence only: reasoning, policies, and outreach are tested against synthetic English records. The prototype does not interpret Tamil posts, audio, video, images, or other multilingual source content.
      • One eval case at a time: each eval row makes a separate model call. The production-style Run All flow sends the 50-case synthetic queue as one bounded batch request.
      • Exact data format required: inputs must follow the embedded creator, source, tracker, and research-history field names and record-ID conventions. There is no general CSV mapper or malformed-file repair step.
      • Human-reviewed drafts only: the prototype does not contact creators, send messages, approve partnerships, or provide a shared production audit database. API-key storage and persisted eval evidence remain in the viewer's browser.
